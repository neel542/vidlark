How to use your phone as a webcam on a Mac, free

Use any iPhone or Android phone as a webcam on a Mac, free, over Wi-Fi with no app to install and no cable. Film up to four angles at once with Vidlark.

By Neel Madhav · 9 October 2026 · 2 min read

Vidlark is free and open source, for Mac and Windows. How to get it

A phone's camera is often sharper than the one in your laptop. With Vidlark, a free recorder for the Mac, you can use your phone as a webcam over your Wi-Fi, whether it is an iPhone or an Android phone. You scan a QR code, and it films. There is no app to install and no cable.

You can use one phone as your main camera, or add up to four phones as extra angles. Each one is saved as its own file, lined up by sound.

What you need

  • A Mac with Vidlark on it. How to use it has the install steps.
  • An iPhone or an Android phone, on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac.
  • A stand for the phone, and its charger for a long take.

Connect the phone

  1. Open the QR code. Press + Add, above the Sources list, then Add a phone with a QR code.
  2. Scan it. Point the phone's camera at the code and tap the link that appears.
  3. Get past the one-time warning. The first time, the phone says the connection is not private. That is expected: the link is Vidlark's own, and it only works on your Wi-Fi. On an iPhone, tap Show Details, then visit this website, then Visit Website. On Android, tap Advanced, then Proceed.
  4. Pick the shape and start. Wide 16:9 is for YouTube, and Tall 9:16 is for Shorts and Reels. Tap Start camera, then Allow.
  5. Check the Mac. The window says Connected. Press Done.

Record with it

The phone is now a source called Camera 2, and it records its own file, camera-2.mov. Press the red button as usual. Every angle is lined up with your main camera by sound, so you pick the angle when you edit.

The picture keeps its shape however the phone turns. Keep the page open on the phone during the take. To film with the phone as your main camera instead, click the Camera row and pick Phone over Wi-Fi.

Using an iPhone and the same Apple Account as your Mac? Apple's Continuity Camera gives the sharpest picture. In Vidlark, Settings, Connect a camera walks you through it.

Questions

Do I need to install an app on the phone?

No. The phone opens a page from your Mac in its web browser. There is no app to install and no account, on an iPhone or an Android phone.

What quality does the phone film at?

Up to 1080p at 30 frames a second, Wide 16:9 or Tall 9:16. Each phone is saved as its own file, lined up with your main camera by sound.

Does this work on Windows?

Not yet. Phones are not in the Windows beta yet. On Windows, Vidlark records your webcam, your mics and your screen.

Get Vidlark free

A free, open source recorder for Mac and Windows. One button records your camera, mic and screen, and makes the finished video. No account, no watermark, no time limit.

Get it on GitHub How to install it

On a Mac: built free from its code, by Claude Code or by hand. On Windows 11: a beta zip to download.
