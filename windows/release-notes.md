**This is a beta.** Vidlark for Windows is new. It records your face, your voice and your screen, but it has only been tried on a few PCs so far. Tell us what goes wrong, and keep a copy of any take that matters.

## Download and open it

1. Download **Vidlark-Windows.zip** below.
2. Right-click the zip, choose **Extract All**, then **Extract**.
3. Open the **Vidlark** folder and double-click **Vidlark.exe**.
4. The first time, Windows says "Windows protected your PC". That is because Vidlark is new and not signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

It needs Windows 11, 64-bit. There is nothing else to install.

## What works

- Your camera and microphone, recorded as camera.mov, after a 3, 2, 1 countdown.
- More microphones, each recorded to its own file.
- **Share screen** during a take: the whole screen or one window. Vidlark then shrinks to a small box with Me, Screen, the time and Stop.
- **Me** puts your camera across the whole screen. **Screen** shows the screen, with your face in a round bubble in the corner. You can drag the bubble, or take it out.
- The computer's own sound, such as a video you play, if you tick it. It can be switched on and off during the take.
- Vidlark's own windows never show in the video.
- After Stop, Vidlark lines up the camera and the screen and makes the finished video, video.mp4, with chapters and a short report.
- If the PC crashes, everything up to about the last 2 seconds is kept.

Takes are saved in `C:\Users\Public\Videos\Vidlark Recordings`.

## Not in this beta yet

- The transcript: it needs a speech model that this beta does not download yet, so takes finish without one.
- Phones as cameras or microphones, and the live view.
- The prompter, face framing (the bubble shows the middle of the camera picture), and the Recordings and Settings pages.
- Sound from one app only. It is all of the computer's sound, or none.
